# Sip — Product Brief

## What this is

A water-drinking companion for a phone. You set a daily goal, tap when you drink
a glass, and a blob character grows and cheers as the day fills up. If you have
not had a drink for a while it reminds you.

## Who it is for

One person who wants to drink more water and will not tolerate friction to do
it. Not a health platform, not a habit tracker with a subscription. The measure
of success is that logging a glass takes one tap and the app is opened without
being nagged into it.

## Why the blob

Because a progress bar is a fact and a character is a reason to come back. The
blob is the difference between an app you check and an app you visit. It grows
with the day's progress and celebrates when the goal is met, and that is the
whole of its job — it holds no data and makes no decisions.

## What matters most

**Logging must be instant.** One tap, persisted immediately. Anything that makes
the user wait or think is a failure of the product regardless of what else works.

**Reminders must be welcome.** A reminder at the wrong moment is worse than none.
Quiet hours are respected at the moment a reminder is scheduled rather than after
it fires, and reminders stop once the goal is met.

**Nothing leaves the device.** No account, no server, no analytics, no network
call. This is a personal app about a bodily function; there is nothing here worth
uploading and every reason not to.

## What is deliberately absent

No accounts or multi-device sync. No social features, streak sharing or
leaderboards. No integration with health platforms. No advertising, no purchases,
no subscription. Each of these is a real product decision rather than an omission
to be filled in later: they all add either a server, a credential, or a reason
for the app to want something from the user.

## What "finished" looks like

The user installs it, sets a goal in under a minute, and uses it for a week
without opening the source code or being confused about why a notification did or
did not arrive.
